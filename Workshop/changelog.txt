[h1]0.7.0 - a deeper AI personality[/h1]
[list]
[*] New: Meridian now develops a slow-forming trait on top of rapport - Meticulous or Neglected from how well the data center is kept up, Steady or Anxious from how often it glitches.
[*] Effects stay small and harmless: Meticulous trims wear a further 3%, Steady makes glitches 10% rarer still. The trait shows on its inspect text and status report, and colours its idle chatter.
[*] A new request, a self-calibration pass, doubles how fast a trait forms for a few days.
[/list]

[h1]0.6.0 - market dynamics[/h1]
[list]
[*] New: each data cartridge type's price now drifts slowly within a band (85-120% of base value) instead of sitting still.
[*] Occasional market events layer on top of one type for a few days: a rival buyer or shortage pushes its price up (+15-35%), a glut pushes it down (-15-30%). Only one event at a time.
[*] Current conditions are shown on the Operations Console's inspect text.
[/list]

[h1]0.5.0 - data contracts[/h1]
[list]
[*] New: once a server rack is running, a buyer occasionally offers a timed data contract - deliver N cartridges of a given type within a deadline for a 20-40% silver bonus over market value.
[*] Accept, decline, or let the offer lapse - nothing is ever taken from you for saying no. Fulfilling one drops the silver bonus near your trade spot; missing the deadline just expires it, no penalty.
[/list]

[h1]0.4.0 update - RimWorld 1.6 support[/h1]
[list]
[*] Added a real RimWorld 1.6 build, packaged alongside the existing 1.5 build - the mod now supports both versions from one download.
[*] RimWorld 1.6 changed a few base-game APIs this mod uses; verified with the full 430-check self-test suite, run twice clean against a real 1.6 install and again against 1.5.
[/list]

[h1]0.3.0 - the AI core[/h1]
[list]
[*] New building: the AI core ("Meridian"), unlocked by Cognitive Computing. It monitors the whole data center by itself (no operations shifts needed), forecasts when each rack needs service, and writes a real status report on request.
[*] Directives: Balanced, Efficiency, Stewardship and Curiosity. Each trades something for something else.
[*] Rapport: it gets on better with you when the data center is healthy. Its benefits scale with rapport.
[*] Requests: every week or two a letter with a small choice (lend compute to research, overclock window, maintenance window).
[*] Small, harmless risks only: a reboot, a day of sulking, or one rack losing its current cartridge progress. Nothing hostile. Security certification halves how often.
[*] Adaptive Learning research: stronger AI, rarer glitches. New "AI core down" alert.
[*] No network access, no language model: every line is a translatable string.
[*] Saves from 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 load unchanged.
[/list]

[h1]0.2.0 - upgrade tree, research uplink and security[/h1]
[list]
[*] Nine follow-up research projects: Optimized Firmware, Immersion Cooling, Predictive Maintenance, Network Fabric, Redundant Power, Distributed Computing, Autonomous Operations, Access Control, Threat Screening and Secure Certification. Every effect is an XML value.
[*] Research uplink: each running rack adds research speed for all your colonists (+4% each up to +20%; Distributed Computing raises it to +7% each up to +45%).
[*] Biometric Access Door: a powered facial-scanner door that only colony members can open.
[*] Metal Detector Gate: colony members and unarmed visitors pass, anyone carrying a weapon is turned away.
[*] Access log, message, sound and an Access denied alert. Scanners fail secure without power.
[*] Secure Certification: with a working door of each kind, data cartridges sell for 20% more.
[*] Saves from 0.1.0 load unchanged. Still RimWorld 1.5 only, with no DLC, no Harmony and no other mods required.
[/list]

[h1]0.1.0 - initial release[/h1]
[list]
[*] Research project: Data Center Infrastructure.
[*] Server Rack, Network Core, Precision Cooling Unit, UPS Unit and Operations Console, plus the Data Cartridge item.
[*] Racks need power, a Network Core link and a safe temperature; wear and heat slow them down and eventually stop them.
[*] New "Data center" work type: operations shifts and rack servicing use Intellectual and Construction skill.
[*] Alerts, messages, status lights, sounds, inspect text, debug actions and an in-game self-test.
[*] Supports RimWorld 1.5. No DLC, no Harmony, no other mods required.
[/list]
